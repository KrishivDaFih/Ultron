ULTRON - Terms and privacy summary
Last updated 6 October 2026

ULTRON is a free, personal, non-commercial project made by Kris, an independent student developer in India.

PRIVACY
- The developer receives none of your data. There are no analytics, ads or trackers.
- Your account, chats and settings are stored on this PC, in the app's data folder.
- Passwords are stored only as a salted one-way hash (PBKDF2-SHA-256).
- Your messages are sent directly to the AI provider you choose (Anthropic, OpenAI or Groq) using your own API key. That provider's privacy policy applies.
- API keys are encrypted with Windows data protection where available.
- You can download or permanently delete your data at any time from Account & privacy inside the app.

AGE
You must be at least 13. Under-18s need a parent or guardian's consent, as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 requires.

TERMS
- The app is provided free and as is. There are no purchases, subscriptions or hidden fees.
- AI usage is billed by your chosen provider to your own API key.
- AI replies can be wrong. Check anything important yourself and don't rely on the app for medical, legal, financial or safety decisions.
- Don't use the app for anything illegal or harmful, or against your AI provider's usage policies.
- What you write is yours.
- To the extent the law allows, the developer isn't liable for losses from using the app, including lost data.
- These terms are governed by the laws of India.

The names Jarvis, Vision, Ultron and E.V. refer to Marvel characters. This is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Marvel, Disney or Anthropic.

The full privacy policy, terms, refund policy, cookie and storage policy, accessibility statement and licences are inside the app under Account & privacy.
